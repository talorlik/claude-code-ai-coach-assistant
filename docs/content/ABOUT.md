# About Studio Itai

## Built for people who want more than another workout plan

Studio Itai was created for people who are ready to train with purpose, structure, and accountability.

Not just to sweat for an hour.

Not just to follow random exercises.

Not just to “try again” every few months.

The studio was built around a simple belief: real progress happens when professional training, personal attention, and consistent guidance work together. A good trainer does not only count repetitions. A good trainer understands the person behind the goal - the schedule, the habits, the injuries, the doubts, the motivation, and the life happening outside the gym.

That is the foundation of Studio Itai.

## The story

Before Studio Itai became a training platform, it started with one trainer, one notebook, and a pattern that kept repeating.

Clients would arrive motivated. Some wanted strength. Some wanted weight loss. Some wanted to move without pain. Some wanted confidence. Some were returning after years away from training. Others had already tried programs, apps, gyms, diets, and routines that looked good on paper but failed in real life.

The problem was rarely effort.

The problem was that most people were given plans without context.

A generic plan does not know that someone works late three nights a week. It does not know that a client has knee discomfort, limited equipment, low confidence, or only thirty minutes between work and family responsibilities. It does not notice when someone disappears for two weeks. It does not explain technique when the trainer is not nearby. It does not adjust when life changes.

Itai saw that the strongest results came from a different kind of coaching: clear structure, careful personalization, honest tracking, and direct support between sessions.

So Studio Itai evolved.

What began as personal coaching became a smarter training experience - a digital fitness platform that combines the discipline of professional coaching with the accessibility of AI-powered support.

## What we do

Studio Itai helps clients build personalized workout routines based on their goals, fitness level, schedule, limitations, preferred training location, and available equipment.

Each client receives a structured workout plan designed around real constraints, not ideal conditions. The platform helps clients follow the plan, track completed workouts, write notes after training, and get immediate answers from an AI virtual trainer when they need guidance.

The result is a more connected coaching experience.

The trainer sees progress.

The client receives clarity.

The plan stays visible.

The work becomes measurable.

## Our training philosophy

Training should be serious, but not confusing.

A strong plan should tell the client exactly what to do, why it matters, and how to perform it safely. It should include the right amount of challenge, enough recovery, and enough flexibility to survive real life.

Studio Itai is built around five principles:

### Personalization

Every client starts with onboarding that captures goals, current fitness level, physical limitations, available days, preferred workout environment, equipment, and personal notes. The plan is built from the person outward, not from a template downward.

### Professional structure

Workouts are organized clearly: exercises, sets, repetitions or duration, rest times, instructions, and safety notes. Clients should never feel lost when opening their plan.

### Accountability

Progress is tracked. Completed workouts are logged. Notes are saved. Consistency becomes visible, both to the client and to the trainer.

### Human attention

Technology supports the coach. It does not replace the coach. Itai can monitor client progress, review workout logs, read training notes, check AI chat history, and contact clients directly when personal guidance is needed.

### Safe progress

The platform respects limitations and injuries. It does not pretend to diagnose medical issues. When pain, injury, or medical concerns appear, the guidance stays responsible and encourages professional care when needed.

## Why AI is part of the experience

AI is used to make coaching faster, clearer, and more responsive.

It helps generate structured workout plans from onboarding data. It helps answer client questions between sessions. It helps explain exercises, suggest safer alternatives, and keep the client connected to the plan.

But AI is not treated as magic.

Every AI-generated plan must follow a predictable structure. Every answer should be based on the client’s goal, fitness level, current plan, and known limitations. The system is designed to support responsible training, not reckless automation.

The purpose is simple: give clients faster help while keeping the trainer informed.
